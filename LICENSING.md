## Not covered

- `src/`: C and C++ reconstructed from the retail executable of PK: Out of the Shadows. The game and its code belong to their rights holders; this repository grants no licence to them.
- `config/eu/`: the retail executable's checksum, its split layout and the symbol list, all describing the game's binary. Names taken from the GameCube release's symbol table are listed there as evidence.
- `docs/img/game/`: emulator screenshots of the game.
- `docs/progress/`: generated progress data and map.
- The Markdown documentation (`README.md`, `disks/README.md`, `docs/*.md`): not licensed for now.
- `tools/requirements.lock`, `tools/toolchain.sha256`: generated lists of pinned versions and hashes.
- Sony's SDK and the C runtime: most of the 1,212 functions in the estimated library ranges are linked from assembly split out of your own copy of the retail executable, never stored here; the three library stubs reconstructed in C (in `src/`) are outside this grant too.
- The CodeWarrior compiler kits: never part of this repository. `tools/setup.sh` downloads them for local use.
- Third-party files: `include/macro.inc` is generated by splat, under its own MIT notice in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
