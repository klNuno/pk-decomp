## Game content

| Topic | Known | Status | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scientists | Regular levels have six captured scientists, each with a rescue timer. Rescuing 40 reportedly opens the final Mothership levels. [Mothership unlock](mysteries.md#question-4) · [Rescue timer](mysteries.md#question-6) | Reported | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PK:_Out_of_the_Shadows) |

[References in PS2 levels](mysteries.md#question-9) | File analysis | [RaymanPC forum](https://web.archive.org/web/20240322204454/https://raymanpc.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=25384) |
