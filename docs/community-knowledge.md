# Community findings

Published observations and claims about the PlayStation 2 release, with their sources.

## Builds and versions

| Topic | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Build dates | North American v1.00 dates to July 16, 2002. European v1.01 dates to August 22, 2002. | Redump [North America](http://redump.org/disc/26896/) and [Europe](http://redump.org/disc/4515/) |
| Release dates | Europe received the game on October 4, 2002, and North America on November 1, 2002. | [The Cutting Room Floor](https://web.archive.org/web/20260923002328/https://tcrf.net/Disney%27s_PK:_Out_of_the_Shadows_(PlayStation_2)) |
| Regional differences | The Cutting Room Floor reports that the European PS2 version adds enemy death sounds also heard in the GameCube releases. The page flags development text and lists build dates. | [The Cutting Room Floor](https://web.archive.org/web/20260923002328/https://tcrf.net/Disney%27s_PK:_Out_of_the_Shadows_(PlayStation_2)) |
| Voice languages | Redump lists English, French, German and Italian for both discs, plus Spanish for Europe. | Redump [North America](http://redump.org/disc/26896/) and [Europe](http://redump.org/disc/4515/) |
| May 2002 prototype | Hidden Palace released a May 2, 2002 CD-R prototype in 2021 as part of Project Deluge. It starts at a level select screen. During play, R1 + R2 opens a menu and L2 + R2 opens a graphics test menu that Select closes. | [Hidden Palace](https://web.archive.org/web/20240512082002/https://hiddenpalace.org/PK:_Out_of_the_Shadows_(May_2,_2002_prototype)), [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/PKOutoftheShadowsMay22002prototype) |
| Engine | Raymap, a viewer for Ubi Soft's OpenSpace engine, includes a preset for the GameCube release. | [Raymap settings](https://github.com/byvar/raymap/blob/master/Assets/Scripts/OpenSpace/General/Settings.cs) |

## Game content

| Topic | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Scientists | Regular levels have six captured scientists, each with a rescue timer. Rescuing 40 reportedly opens the final Mothership levels. | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PK:_Out_of_the_Shadows) |
| Cheat mode | An unverified submission claims that pressing X, Square, Up, Down, Left, Right, Left, Right, X, Square while under fire gives PK unlimited health and a stronger gun after death. | [Neoseeker](https://web.archive.org/web/20181125001920/http://www.neoseeker.com/disney-pk-shadows/cheats/ps2/) |
| Music storage | A forum analysis describes PS2 music as ADPCM loops in `DATA.HST`. The `M_*.HXD` files define playback orders, including unused ones. | [RaymanPC forum](https://web.archive.org/web/20240322204454/https://raymanpc.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=25384) |
| Unused music | The same thread reports PS2 loops and track sections absent from the GameCube files, including material discussed in connection with Area 52. | [RaymanPC forum](https://web.archive.org/web/20240322204454/https://raymanpc.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=25384) |
